Khata Web App

Khata is a web-based shop management application designed to simplify daily business operations for shopkeepers.

Key Features

- Shop Management: Create and manage your own shop.
- Inventory Management: Maintain product and stock records.
- Sales Management: Record and track daily sales.
- Customer Management: Maintain customer profiles and records.
- Udhar Management: Track credit transactions, outstanding balances, and payment records.

Purpose

The main goal of Khata is to provide shopkeepers with a simple and organized digital solution for managing their business activities and maintaining accurate financial records.
